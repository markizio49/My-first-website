Breathing

  • While there is air around, the suit uses no blood
  • Under water or below 50% oxygen it breathes on blood
  • Without blood, the suit severely slows movement and deals 2 internal damage to the torso every 2 seconds. Insert a vial containing blood or remove the suit to stop the damage.

Thalamus Gene

Destroy a Thalamus Brain in an infested wreck and refine the material it drops with stabilozine at a Research Station. Insert the refined gene into a Gene Splicer and equip the splicer in the health interface.

An enemy NPC killed by a carrier with the gene active rises after 8 seconds and fights on the carrier’s side. The ally dies 2–5 minutes later (longer with a higher-quality gene). Each target can be raised once per round.

Reanimation requires LuaCs for Barotrauma on the multiplayer server or in the single-player client.

Latcher Gene

Found when researching unidentified genetic material at a research station, like the regular genes. Load it into a gene splicer and the carrier grows a long, barbed tongue.

The attack key (R by default) shoots the tongue up to 10 m. It pierces the target (up to three limbs), causes lacerations, bleeding and concussion, stuns it and holds it for 2.5 seconds, dragging it towards the carrier, then reels back. Cooldown: 8 seconds. A small husk appendage grows on the carrier’s head. This gene does not need LuaCs.

Leviathan

A living deep-sea exosuit grown over a vanilla one: dark carapace, glowing blue glands, fins. An Abyssal Heart beats where the fuel rod would go (about 30 minutes each), and alien blood provides oxygen and feeds its organ weapons.

Organ weapons


Needle Volley — about 10 bone needles per second.


Acid Spit — a ~3 m stream of acid that eats through flesh and armor.

The Volley and Spit regrow their reserve on their own while the wearer has a Leviathan with blood on, costing about 1 blood per second.

Gebira

A long abyssal sea serpent with ribbon fins. It circles submarines in the depths and snatches divers outside the hull.

Its carcass yields alien blood, swim bladders and unresearched genetic material, which can be researched into the Latcher gene.

Kramer Prototype

A failed experiment in growing living gear: a pale bony skull and shoulders, a dark ribbed body, long blade-like legs and spikes along its spine. Tougher than a husk chimera (900 health), but it doesn't carry the husk infection and isn't friendly with husks.

Its carcass yields alien blood, unresearched genetic material. It doesn't appear in the campaign yet: spawn KramerPrototype cursor.
