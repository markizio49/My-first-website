Welcome to Biopunk for Barotrauma

The mod adds a living suit grown from Thalamus genes and fed on alien blood, plus symbiote weapons that only awaken in the hands of a well-fed host. Everything is built on vanilla items and mechanics.

Current mod version: 0.0.1 alpha · game 1.13.5.0

Breathing

  • While there is air around, the suit uses no blood
  • Under water or below 50% oxygen it breathes on blood
  • Without blood it slows you down heavily

Materials

Where to find

  • Genetic material: Thalamus Brain in an infested wreck, always
  • Stabilozine: Medical Fabricator, shops
  • Strange Egg: Peanut, Psilotoad, Orangeboy, Balloon; cabinets and shops
  • Diver's Remains: storage and mineral cabinets
  • Alien Blood: monsters, Thalamus organs
  • Needle Gland: in a dead spineling's inventory

About

Biopunk for Barotrauma brings biopunk to Europa: gear is not built at a fabricator, it is grown from genetic material and fed on monster blood.

The mod only uses vanilla resources: alien blood, stabilozine, pet eggs and diver's remains.
